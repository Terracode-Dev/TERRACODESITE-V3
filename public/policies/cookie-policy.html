Cookie Policy

2 August 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to collect information about your browsing activity. They help us provide, secure, and improve our Services.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for core website functions such as account login, secure payment processing, and session management.

These cannot be disabled.

Examples: Authentication tokens, session cookies.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

Collect information about how users interact with our Services (e.g., pages visited, time spent, errors encountered).

Used to improve website performance and user experience.

Examples: Google Analytics, error tracking tools.

Functional Cookies

Enable enhanced features such as remembering preferences, language settings, and customized layouts.

Examples: Saved login details, theme preferences.

Advertising & Targeting Cookies

Used to deliver relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness.

These may be placed by Terracode or trusted third-party partners.

Examples: Google Ads, social media pixels.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party service providers we integrate with, including payment gateways, analytics platforms, and cloud services.

These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies.

3. Cookie Retention

Session Cookies expire when you close your browser.

Persistent Cookies remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted.

Retention periods vary by cookie type (typically 1 day to 24 months).

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser or device settings. Options include:

Blocking all cookies

Blocking third-party cookies only

Deleting existing cookies

Please note: disabling cookies may affect functionality of our Services, including logins, payments, and personalization.

5. Consent

For regions where required (e.g., EU/EEA), we use a cookie consent banner. By continuing to use our Services, you consent to the use of cookies in line with this policy.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about cookies or this policy, contact us at operations@terracodedev.com.
