Refund Policy

2 December 2024

1. SaaS Subscriptions

Free Trials: If a free trial is provided, charges begin only after the trial period ends.

Monthly Subscriptions: Refunds are not generally issued for partial months of service. If you cancel, access continues until the end of the billing cycle.

Annual Subscriptions: Refunds may be requested within 14 days of the initial purchase. After this period, refunds will not be issued, but you may cancel renewal.

Billing Errors: If you are charged incorrectly (e.g., duplicate payments), we will issue a full refund of the erroneous charge.

2. Custom Software Services

Upfront Deposits: Deposits paid to initiate a project are non-refundable, as they secure development resources.

Milestone Payments: Once a milestone has been completed and delivered, refunds are not applicable.

Project Cancellations: If a project is canceled mid-way, we will assess work completed and may issue a partial refund for unspent, unused hours.

3. Exceptions

Refunds may also be granted in the following circumstances:

Fraudulent or unauthorized charges proven with documentation.

Inability to deliver Services due to issues on Terracode’s side.

Technical issues preventing SaaS access, where Terracode cannot provide a resolution.

4. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at operations@terracodedev.com with transaction details. Refund requests are typically processed within 7–14 business days.

5. Non-Refundable Items

Setup fees, onboarding costs, or third-party service fees are non-refundable.

Services or SaaS products purchased through resellers or partners must follow their refund policies.
