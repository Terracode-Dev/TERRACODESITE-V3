Service Level Agreement (SLA)

2 December 2024

1. Service Availability

Terracode guarantees 99.9% uptime per calendar month for SaaS products, excluding scheduled maintenance.

Scheduled Maintenance: We will provide at least 48 hours’ notice for planned maintenance that may affect availability.

Exclusions: Downtime caused by force majeure events, third-party failures (e.g., internet providers, payment gateways), or customer misuse is not counted against uptime.

2. Support Commitments

Terracode provides enterprise-grade technical support with the following response time targets:

Support channels include email, ticketing system, and dedicated account manager (enterprise plans only).

3. Escalation Path

If resolution targets are not met, issues will escalate to:

Tier 1: Support Engineer

Tier 2: Senior Engineer

Tier 3: Engineering Manager

Tier 4: CTO/Executive Oversight

4. Service Credits

If uptime falls below guaranteed levels, customers may request service credits:

Credits are applied to future invoices and cannot exceed 50% of one month’s fee.

Customers must request credits within 30 days of the incident.

5. Customer Responsibilities

Maintain stable internet connectivity and updated browsers/software.

Provide accurate information when reporting issues.

Follow best practices for security and account management.

6. SLA Exclusions

This SLA does not apply to:

Beta or trial features

Third-party software or integrations

Issues caused by customer’s hardware, networks, or misuse

Force majeure events beyond Terracode’s control

7. Governing Law

This SLA is governed by the same jurisdiction as Terracode’s Terms & Conditions.
